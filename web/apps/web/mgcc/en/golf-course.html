Golf Course Facilities.

Modern Golf & Country Club displays an amazing layout. The 6,350 meters course is a masterpiece of five-time British Open Champion, Peter Thomson, that features a magnificent view and designed for golfers with various levels of skill. An 18 holes Championship course completed with exclusive lobby, indoor and outdoor driving range, pro shop, chipping green, bunker shot and practice putting green, men & ladies locker rooms, hot and cold plunge pools and sauna
